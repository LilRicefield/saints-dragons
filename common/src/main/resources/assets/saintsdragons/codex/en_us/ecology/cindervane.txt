Cindervanes are not conquerors, nor guardians. They are carriers. Early trade routes, evacuation corridors, and messenger networks were built around known Cindervane migratory paths. Their eggs, scattered rather than hoarded, suggest deliberate dispersion, either by the dragons themselves or by civilizations that understood their value as mobile lifelines rather than weapons. Cindervanes lack complex combat instincts not due to weakness, but specialization. They do not linger in battle. When threatened, they escalate vertically, ignite, and end the encounter in a single decisive descent. Survival over supremacy.
