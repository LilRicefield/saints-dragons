Ignivoruses are primordial firebound titans whose presence reshapes environments through sheer thermal dominance. Unlike Raevyx, whose authority is enforced through restraint, Ignivoruses exert control by occupation. Where one settles, the land does not recover. Native primarily to the Nether, Ignivoruses are believed to have originated in extreme volcanic ecosystems where constant exposure to magma and hellfire selected for organisms capable of converting ambient heat into sustained power. Their rare appearances in the Overworld are not migratory, but anomalous, often linked to ancient interdimensional traversal or forced displacement. Ignivoruses are not mindless engines of destruction. They are patient and acutely aware of hierarchy. Aggression is not constant, but once provoked, escalation is absolute. An Ignivorus does not retreat. It overwhelms.
