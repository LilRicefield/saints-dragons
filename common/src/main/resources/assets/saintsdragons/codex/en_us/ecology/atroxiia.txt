Unlike most dragons, Atroxiia evolved as terrestrial lindworms, relying on powerful musculature and extraordinary flexibility instead of wings. Their bodies channel primordial frost through the tail, reshaping the battlefield with overwhelming precision rather than reckless ferocity. Despite their imposing nature, they are remarkably composed, engaging only when challenged or provoked. Those who earn an Atroxiia's allegiance do not tame a beast, but are acknowledged by an ancient sovereign.
